Questions

How much does a furnace repair cost?

It depends on what's wrong. We tell you the price before we start any repair.

Should I repair or replace my furnace?

If the repair makes sense, we'll do it. If your furnace is old or the repair costs too much, we'll tell you straight and quote a replacement. New furnace installation starts at $2,199.

How much does a new furnace cost?

New furnace installation starts at $2,199. Larger and higher-efficiency models cost more. We confirm your exact price in writing at a free in-home visit, before any work begins.

Can you install it the same day?

In many cases, yes. It depends on the model and your home. We confirm timing when we quote.

What does the 10-year labour warranty cover?

Every furnace we install has a 10-year labour warranty, on top of the manufacturer's equipment warranty. We go over the details with you at your visit.
